## About FORESE

FORESE (Forum for Economic Studies by Engineers) is a student-run placement and industry-interaction forum at Sri Venkateswara College of Engineering. The club bridges academic learning with real-world opportunities through industry interactions, placement simulations, employability programmes, higher-education initiatives, workshops, and practical learning.

FORESE focuses on helping students develop the skills and confidence required for the next stage of their careers, including communication, teamwork, problem-solving, interview readiness, and professional awareness.

## Flagship Initiative — Mock Placements

**FORESE Mock Placements** is the flagship annual initiative of FORESE, designed to give pre-final-year students an authentic simulation of the recruitment process before their actual placement season.

The programme recreates key stages of recruitment, including:

1. **Aptitude**
2. **Group Discussion**
3. **Personal Interview**
4. **Expert Feedback**
5. **Resume Evaluation**
6. **Practical Placement Preparation**

The event is conducted in both online and offline formats and is open to pre-final-year students across disciplines.

## FORED — Foreign Universities Education Fair

**7 August 2026**  
**In collaboration with Edwise**

FORESE hosted its first **Foreign Universities Education Fair (FORED)** for the academic year 2026–2027, creating a platform for pre-final and final-year students to explore higher-education opportunities abroad.

Representatives from international universities, including:

- **The University of Sydney**
- **The University of Glasgow**
- **The University of Nottingham**

Students participated in one-to-one interactions with university delegates and gained practical information about:

- Courses and programmes
- Eligibility requirements
- University admission processes
- GRE and GMAT
- TOEFL and IELTS
- Scholarships
- Tuition and living costs
- Planning for applications to universities abroad

The initiative helped students develop a clearer understanding of the international higher-education landscape and the practical requirements involved in pursuing postgraduate education overseas.

## LEAP — Learners Employability Awareness Programme

**28 July – 7 August 2026**

FORESE conducted **LEAP (The Learners Employability Awareness Programme)**, a series of talks focused on employability and professional readiness.

The programme addressed questions such as:

- What do employers expect from graduates?
- Which skills matter during recruitment?
- How can students prepare before placement season?
- How can students translate academic knowledge into workplace readiness?

The initiative complemented FORESE's placement activities by helping students understand employability beyond academic performance.
